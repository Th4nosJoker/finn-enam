# Industrial-company source review

These are prepared primary-source research dossiers, not claims of live API generation. All citation quotes were automatically checked against whitespace-normalized original page text. No broker notes, news, or outside company knowledge were used.

## Important interpretation limits

- Titan statutory Q2 FY2027 PBT/PAT of ₹3,000/₹2,393 crore include a ₹671 crore Damas put-option liability credit. Business-highlight PBT/PAT use adjusted figures. Bullion/digi-gold exclusions also change the revenue basis.
- Titan annual standalone ratios are not reused as consolidated ratios. Gold-on-loan, lease liabilities and borrowings remain distinct.
- Polycab values are INR million, with total group PAT distinguished from parent-attributable PAT. The report’s consolidated ratio table differs from some headline KPIs; this dossier does not silently substitute them.
- Polycab FY2030 EBITDA targets are not scored against single-quarter EBIT. Titan’s broad jewellery margin anchor is not treated as a precise quarterly promise. All four-quarter rows are honestly not assessable when a matched commitment is unavailable.
- FCF calculations deduct explicitly identified cash-capex lines; they are analytical measures, not company-reported FCF.
